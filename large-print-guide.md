# UPI and Digital Payment Safety
## A plain-language guide for senior citizens and families

*Approx. 600 words; headings and checklist are included in the count depending on the word-count tool. Confirm the final count before submission.*

### Your money deserves time and care

UPI makes it possible to send money from a phone. It is useful, but every payment deserves attention. You do not have to act quickly because a caller tells you to. Stop if you feel confused or pressured. It is safe to end an unexpected call and check with someone you trust.

### Keep secret codes private

Your UPI PIN is secret. An OTP is a one-time code used to confirm certain actions. Never tell a caller your PIN, OTP or password, even if the person claims to be from a bank, police office, payment app or customer-care team. A genuine helper should not ask you to reveal your secret codes. Do not write these codes on the phone or share them in a message.

### Check before paying

Before you approve a payment, read the recipient name and amount on the screen. Ask yourself: Do I know this person? Did I start this payment? Is the amount correct? If anything looks unfamiliar, cancel or stop and verify. Never enter a UPI PIN just to receive money. Scanning a QR code and entering a PIN can authorize a payment, so do not follow instructions from a stranger who says a scan is needed for a refund or prize.

### Beware of urgent messages

A message may say your bank account will be blocked today unless you update KYC using a link. Another caller may say you have won a prize, need to pay a fee, or must install an app to fix a payment. These can be warning signs. Do not click unexpected links, install apps sent by strangers, or allow screen sharing or remote access because an unknown caller asks you to. Do not rely on a phone number supplied by the caller. Use the official bank app or website to find help.

### Verify people and requests

If someone says they are a relative using a new number and asks for urgent money, pause. Call the relative on a number already saved in your contacts. If you receive an unexpected request from a business or organisation, check it independently using official contact details. Never let a stranger stop you from checking with your family.

### If you think you made a mistake

Do not panic or hide the incident. Contact your bank or payment provider promptly through official channels. Keep relevant messages and transaction details, but do not post private information publicly. In India, if you suspect financial cyber fraud, call **1930 promptly** and report through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at **https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/**. Follow the current instructions on the official site.

### Ask for help safely

A trusted family member can practise with you using a demonstration or screenshots rather than a real payment. They should explain each step and let you remain in control. Never reveal your PIN or OTP, even to a family member. If a caller makes you uncomfortable, end the call.

## Remember
**STOP. CHECK THE NAME AND AMOUNT. KEEP YOUR PIN AND OTP SECRET. VERIFY THROUGH OFFICIAL CHANNELS.**

This guide is for awareness, not financial advice. Scams change, so check current information from RBI, NPCI, CERT-In and official cybercrime services.
