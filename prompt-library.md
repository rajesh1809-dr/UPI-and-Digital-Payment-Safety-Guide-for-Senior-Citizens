# Prompt Library (document the prompts you actually run)

Use these prompts in at least two AI research tools and save the outputs/screenshots. AI responses are not authoritative sources.

## Prompt 1 — Research tool A
“You are helping prepare a public digital-safety guide for senior citizens in India. Identify common UPI, OTP, KYC-link, fake customer-care, QR-code and impersonation scam patterns. Search for official RBI, NPCI, CERT-In and cybercrime.gov.in sources. For every factual claim, provide the exact official page URL and publication/update date if shown. Clearly label anything you cannot verify. Do not invent citations or phone numbers. Return a table of claim, source, date, confidence and what still needs checking.”

## Prompt 2 — Independent research tool B
“Independently research digital payment fraud-prevention advice for older adults in India. Prioritize primary official sources from RBI, NPCI, CERT-In and cybercrime.gov.in. Find claims that agree or conflict with another research summary. Do not rely on AI summaries as sources. Flag outdated, unclear or unsupported advice. Include exact page titles, URLs and dates accessed.”

## Prompt 3 — Plain-language guide
“Rewrite the verified facts below for senior citizens with simple sentences, large-print headings and respectful language. Do not add facts or alter the meaning. Keep UPI PIN and OTP distinctions accurate. Mark any claim that needs source verification. Source notes: [paste verified notes].”

## Prompt 4 — Tamil translation draft
“Translate the approved English safety guide into clear, natural Tamil suitable for older adults in Tamil Nadu. Preserve all safety meanings exactly. Do not invent local helpline numbers. Keep official URLs unchanged. Flag technical terms that require a fluent Tamil speaker's review.”

## Prompt 5 — Fictional training samples
“Create six fictional training examples covering fake KYC, QR/refund, fake customer care, OTP request, family impersonation and prize fee. Label every item ‘TRAINING SAMPLE’. Use no real names, phone numbers, banks or clickable scam links. For each sample, list warning signs and the safe response. Do not provide operational instructions that would help a scammer.”

## Prompt 6 — Quiz
“Create eight multiple-choice questions for senior citizens based only on the verified safety notes below. Use plain language, three options per question, one correct answer and a short explanation. Do not use trick wording. Add a source note for factual questions. Verified notes: [paste notes].”

## Prompt 7 — AI output red-team review
“Review this digital-safety material for hallucinated facts, unsafe advice, outdated phone numbers/URLs, ambiguity, accessibility problems, inaccurate translation and claims that lack official evidence. Return a table with exact quote, risk, evidence needed, recommended correction and reviewer decision. Do not assume the text is correct.”

## Prompt 8 — Weekly tip draft
“Draft one short WhatsApp-ready digital payment safety tip for senior citizens in India. Use one topic only, under 70 words, simple language, no fearmongering, no real bank or victim details, and no unverified number. Include an official source URL that must be checked by a human before sharing. Label it DRAFT — VERIFY BEFORE SENDING. Provide English and Tamil versions.”

## Prompt 9 — Before/after analysis
“Compare the original AI draft with the human-verified final version. Identify edits for factual accuracy, safety, clarity, readability, respectful tone and translation quality. Do not claim a correction was made unless it appears in the versions. Output a table with example, reason, and evidence.”

## Prompt 10 — Poster design
“Create a high-contrast, accessible poster for senior citizens about UPI safety. Use very few words, large type, clear simple icons, the message ‘Never share PIN or OTP’, ‘Check name and amount’, and a reminder to call 1930 promptly if financial cyber fraud is suspected in India. Do not include bank logos, real phone numbers other than the official helpline, or claims not supported by official sources. Ensure the text is readable and verify all spelling before printing.”
