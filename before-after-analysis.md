# Before / After Analysis

Use real examples from your own AI output. This table is a template; replace the sample entries with evidence from your tests.

| Area | Before (AI draft) | Review action | After (verified version) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tone | “Act now or you will lose everything!” | Remove fearmongering | “Pause if a message pressures you to act quickly.” | Draft and final screenshots |
| PIN safety | “Enter the PIN to get a refund.” | Flag as unsafe and incorrect | “Never enter a UPI PIN just to receive money.” | NPCI source URL |
| Contact details | AI-generated number or guessed support contact | Delete and use official sources only | Link to official cybercrime portal; helpline checked on official source | URL and access date |
| Translation | Literal or confusing Tamil wording | Fluent speaker review | Clear Tamil sentence with same safety meaning | Reviewer checklist |
| Accessibility | Dense paragraph, small text | Use short sections, headings and high contrast | Large-print guide and responsive website | Screenshot / print test |

## Evaluation questions
1. Did the final version remove unsupported claims?
2. Did all official URLs open and support the statements?
3. Did the translation preserve the safety meaning?
4. Could a senior reader find the main action within 10 seconds?
5. Were any AI mistakes missed? Record them honestly.
