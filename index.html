KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Stop if someone…

🔐

Asks for your PIN or OTP

Keep these secret. A genuine helper should not ask you to reveal them.

⏳

Creates panic or urgency

Claims your account will be blocked unless you act right now.

📲

Sends an unknown link or app

Do not click, install, or give remote access at a stranger’s request.

💸

Says “scan to receive money”

A QR scan and UPI PIN may authorize a payment. Check the screen carefully.
