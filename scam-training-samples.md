# Six Fictional Scam Scripts — Training Samples

**Safety note:** All examples below are fictional training samples. They do not use real phone numbers, banks, victims or links. Do not use these scripts to contact people.

## 1. Fake KYC update
**Training sample message:** “Your account will be blocked today. Open this unknown link and enter your OTP to update KYC.”

**Warning signs:** Threat, urgency, unknown link, OTP request.

**Correct response:** Do not open the link or share an OTP. Open the bank's official app or independently type its verified website address. Contact the bank using official contact details.

## 2. Fake refund by QR code
**Training sample call:** “I am sending you a refund. Scan this QR code and enter your UPI PIN to receive it.”

**Warning signs:** QR scan described as necessary to receive money; PIN request.

**Correct response:** Stop. A QR scan and PIN can authorize a payment. Never enter a PIN just to receive money. Verify the transaction in your payment app.

## 3. Fake customer-care helper
**Training sample call:** “I work in support. Install this screen-sharing app so I can fix your payment right now.”

**Warning signs:** Unsolicited support, app installation, remote access.

**Correct response:** Do not install the app or grant access. Find support details inside the official app or on the institution's official website.

## 4. OTP to stop a transaction
**Training sample call:** “A suspicious payment is happening. Tell me the OTP you received so I can cancel it.”

**Warning signs:** OTP request, fear, caller claims special authority.

**Correct response:** Never disclose the OTP. End the call and contact the bank independently using official contact details.

## 5. Family emergency from a new number
**Training sample message:** “I am your relative using a new number. Send money immediately. Do not call anyone else.”

**Warning signs:** New number, secrecy, urgency, pressure to transfer.

**Correct response:** Call the relative using a previously saved number or verify with another trusted family member before sending anything.

## 6. Prize or benefit fee
**Training sample message:** “You have won a cash prize. Pay a small processing fee through UPI now to claim it.”

**Warning signs:** Unexpected prize, upfront fee, pressure to act.

**Correct response:** Do not pay or share sensitive information. Verify the claim through the organisation's official website. If it cannot be verified, ignore it.

## Facilitator discussion
Ask participants: What is the warning sign? What should the person do first? Which official channel can verify the claim? Remind everyone never to practise using real PINs, OTPs, bank details or transfers.
