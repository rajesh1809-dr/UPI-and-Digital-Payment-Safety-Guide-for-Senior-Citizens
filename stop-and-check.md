# Stop-and-Check Safety Checklist (printable)

## STOP
- Am I being rushed, frightened or threatened?
- Did I receive an unexpected call, link, QR code or payment request?
- Is someone asking for a PIN, OTP, password or screen-sharing access?

## CHECK
- Do I know the person or business I am paying?
- Is the recipient name correct?
- Is the amount correct?
- Did I initiate this payment myself?
- Am I using the official app and a trusted connection?
- Can I verify the request using a known phone number or official website?

## PROTECT
- Never tell anyone your UPI PIN, OTP or password.
- Do not enter a UPI PIN just to receive money.
- Do not install remote-access apps because an unknown caller asks you to.
- Do not trust a caller ID or a forwarded customer-care number by itself.
- If unsure, stop. Ask a trusted person to review the situation without revealing secrets.
- If you suspect financial cyber fraud in India, call **1930 promptly** and report at https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/. Use official sources to confirm current reporting steps.

**Remember:** It is always okay to pause. A real payment does not need to be rushed by a stranger.
