# Five-Slide Family Workshop

## Slide 1 — Pause before you pay
- UPI is useful, but every payment needs a check.
- Do not let a stranger rush you.
- Family members should support, not take control.
**Speaker note:** Invite participants to share general warning signs; do not ask them to disclose personal banking experiences.

## Slide 2 — Secrets stay secret
- Never reveal UPI PIN, OTP or password.
- Never enter a PIN just to receive money.
- Never install remote-access apps at an unknown caller's request.
**Speaker note:** Explain the difference between a payment PIN and a code received by SMS without asking anyone to show a real code.

## Slide 3 — Spot common scam patterns
- Fake KYC/account-block message.
- Fake customer-care caller.
- QR code/refund trick.
- Relative using a new number.
- Prize or benefit fee.
**Speaker note:** Use only the fictional training samples in this repository.

## Slide 4 — Stop-and-check routine
1. Check recipient name.
2. Check amount.
3. Confirm you initiated the transaction.
4. Pause if pressured.
5. Verify independently through official channels.
**Speaker note:** Practise with a mock screenshot or role-play, never with a real transfer.

## Slide 5 — If something goes wrong
- Contact your bank/payment provider using official details.
- In India, call 1930 promptly if financial cyber fraud is suspected.
- Report through https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/.
- Save relevant transaction details and messages securely.
**Speaker note:** Confirm current official procedures before delivering the workshop.
