# Eight-Question Quiz and Answer Key

The interactive quiz is also available on the website.

1. A caller asks for your UPI PIN to receive a refund.  
   **Answer: B — Stop and do not share it.** A PIN can authorize a payment.
2. What should you check before paying by UPI?  
   **Answer: A — Recipient name and amount.**
3. A stranger sends an unknown link to update KYC.  
   **Answer: C — Do not open it; verify through official bank channels.**
4. Someone asks you to install a screen-sharing app to fix UPI.  
   **Answer: B — Refuse and contact official support yourself.**
5. A caller says you must act in one minute or your account closes.  
   **Answer: B — Warning sign; pause and verify.**
6. You suspect money was stolen through cyber fraud in India. Which helpline should you contact promptly?  
   **Answer: A — 1930.** Also use the official cybercrime.gov.in portal and follow current instructions.
7. A relative messages from a new number asking for urgent money.  
   **Answer: B — Verify by calling a known number.**
8. What is the safest way to find your bank's customer-care number?  
   **Answer: B — Use the official bank app or official website.**

## Scoring guide
- 7–8 correct: Strong understanding. Keep using the checklist.
- 5–6 correct: Review the safety guide and try again.
- 0–4 correct: Practise the examples with a trusted person using a demo only.

The score is for learning, not a measure of someone's intelligence or ability.
